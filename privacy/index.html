Privacy Policy

App: Expolog
Effective date: 2026-09-05
Contact: ksc06289@gmail.com

This app helps you record supplier visits at overseas trade shows (questions, recorded answers, photos, notes). There is no account sign-up, and we do not collect information that identifies you, such as your name, email address, or phone number. All records stay on your device by default and are never sent to the developer's servers.

1. Data we store and process

DataWhere it livesPurpose
Trip, company, and visit records (questions, answer text, notes)On your device (app database)Visit record keeping
Voice recordingsOn your deviceAnswer capture; AI transcription, translation, and summary
Photos (booths, business cards, products)On your deviceVisit attachments
(Optional) An OpenAI API key you enter yourselfDevice secure storage (iOS Keychain)High-quality AI processing (Section 3)

The app does not collect location, contacts, or advertising identifiers, and contains no advertising or tracking SDKs.

When you set up offline mode, the app downloads translation and speech-recognition model files from Google (ML Kit) and Hugging Face servers. These are one-way downloads of program files; none of your records are sent.

2. Device permissions

  • Microphone: recording answers during a visit
  • Camera: photographing booths, business cards, and products
  • Photo Library: attaching photos (read) and saving captured photos (add)
  • Speech Recognition: on-device transcription (processed on device; nothing is sent out)

Each permission is requested the first time the feature is used; the rest of the app works if you decline.

3. Optional AI processing (your own API key)

By default, transcription and translation happen entirely on your device, and your data never leaves it.

Only if you enter your own OpenAI API key in Settings, answer recordings are sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) to OpenAI (US) for transcription, translation, and summarization. Results are returned to the app and stored on your device. This transfer happens under your own API agreement with OpenAI, and the API does not use your inputs for AI training. If you remove the key, processing returns fully on-device.

4. Retention and deletion

  • All records are kept only on your device. Deleting records in the app, or uninstalling the app, removes them immediately.
  • The developer operates no servers that store your data.

5. Your rights

You can view, edit, and delete your records in the app at any time.

6. Children

This app is not directed at children under 14.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, we will announce it in the app or on the store page and update the effective date above.
